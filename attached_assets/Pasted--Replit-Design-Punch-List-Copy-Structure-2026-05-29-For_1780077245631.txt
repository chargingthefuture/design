- `MobileServiceCreditsPublic.tsx:22` — "real monetary value" → utility framing.

  "Service Credits" (two words), "SC", "cr", and lowercase "credits" with **ServiceCredits**.
  Affected (non-exhaustive): `ServiceCredits*`, `MobileServiceCredits*`, `SocketRelay.tsx:126`,
  `SocketRelayPublic.tsx:118`, `MobileTrustTransport.tsx:51,74,109`, `LevelUp.tsx:141`,

## 6. Loading states (normalize — content stays)

The slogan stays. Requirement: **calm, no flashing, ADA-compliant, trauma-informed.** Action:
**normalize all loading screens to one consistent rendering** of "EXIT THEIR ECONOMY" / "EXIT THE
PSYOP" (two stacked lines; no literal slash). 40 loaders currently vary only in transcription —
make them identical in markup/spacing. `landing/LandingLoading.tsx` currently uses a different
message ("Preparing your community experience…") — owner has not asked to change the slogan, so
align it to the standard loader unless intentionally kept calmer for the public landing surface
(flag for owner if unsure).
