CONTEXT — what changed in the app (reconcile the mockups to this)

We consolidated two surfaces into ONE. The "feed-announcements" plugin has been
absorbed into the Survivor Hub, which is now the single app HOMEPAGE. There is no
longer a standalone "Feed + Announcements" app — it has been retired/aliased into
the Hub.

The Hub homepage now presents ONE blended, publicly-viewable channel (named
"community") that interleaves three content types in a single chronological stream:

  1. Announcements — authored ONLY by an admin (the owner). No other user can post
     announcements.
  2. AI Q&A — the Hub's AI chat assistant. Users ask questions; the assistant answers
     inline in the same stream.
  3. Peer-to-peer community posts — the ONLY user-authored social surface in the whole
     product. Publicly viewable (even unauthenticated) on purpose: visibility keeps
     behavior good and shows real people benefiting from the economy.

Product stance to reflect everywhere: this is deliberately NOT social media. Keep
user-authored free content minimal and economy-scoped. No separate "Questions" app,
no separate "Announcements" app, no multi-channel sidebar for now — it is a SINGLE
blended channel.
